## Context

Matija wants to apply to Grad Zagreb's 2026 call "Javni poziv za dodjelu potpora male vrijednosti za financiranje projekata korištenja otvorenih podataka za 2026." with a single, ambitious project instead of the earlier "Atlas zagrebačkih udruga": a premium, accessible, real-time "god's view" dashboard of Zagreb built on open data, which a person can use only for a few minutes at a time after scanning a constantly rotating QR code shown on another device or a public screen. The scan unlocks the dashboard on both devices. Payment is interaction and limited usage, not money. Emergency information stays open to everyone; public screens show a readable teaser; anonymous usage data goes to the city under a closed licence. Stage 1 is the dashboard, the pairing mechanic, kiosk screens and the analytics hand-off. A social layer and decentralisation are later stages and are out of this plan.

### C. Pairing and platform primitives (verified from RFC 8628, RFC 6238, NIST SP 800-63B, Cloudflare docs)

- Pattern: OAuth device flow shape (screen shows a code, phone authorises, screen polls or holds a WebSocket), rotating codes every 30 s (TOTP-style), single-use tokens, at least six digits of entropy with rate limiting, 10-minute validity ceiling, and the possession check "the code displayed here must match the code on the other device". QRLJacking (attacker shows a cloned QR) is the known attack; our sessions carry no account or data, so the exposure is low, and single-use plus short rotation bounds it.
- Network distinctness: Workers see `request.cf.asn`, `asOrganization`, `colo`, `city` and `CF-Connecting-IP` on all plans. Carrier-grade NAT (RFC 6598) means many phones share one public IPv4 and one ASN; a café Wi-Fi and a phone on mobile data differ in ASN almost always; two phones on the same carrier may not. IP addresses are personal data (CJEU Breyer): compare in memory, never store.
- Durable Objects: SQLite-backed, WebSocket hibernation (no duration billing while idle), alarms with at-least-once execution for session expiry; paid plan 1 M requests/month included, WebSocket messages billed 20:1. KV TTL minimum 60 s. Rate Limiting API is permissive and eventually consistent, period 10 or 60 s, not for exact accounting. Turnstile tokens single-use, 5-minute validity, invisible mode available, WCAG 2.2 AAA. Workers Analytics Engine: 20 blobs, 20 doubles, one 96-byte index per point, 3-month retention, 10 M points/month included on Paid, currently unbilled.
- Browser: BarcodeDetector works on Chrome Android and macOS, is disabled by default on iOS Safari and absent on Windows and Firefox, so a WASM fallback is required (nimiq/qr-scanner, MIT, BarcodeDetector-first, ~16 kB gzipped; psdlat's scanner already has the `createDecoder` seam). Screen Wake Lock and Fullscreen APIs need HTTPS and a user gesture; kiosk Chromium flags `--kiosk --noerrdialogs --disable-infobars --incognito` (likely). Raspberry Pi 5 2 GB about 65 USD, Touch Display 2 40 to 80 USD (likely, 2026 prices).

### D. Accessibility and privacy constraints (verified)

- WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (Level A): each content time limit must be one of turn-off, adjust (10x), extend (warning at least 20 s before, simple action, at least 10 times), real-time exception, essential exception, or 20 hours. "Essential" requires both that removal would fundamentally change the content and that no conforming alternative exists. The Understanding text: "Content that operates on a timer does not need to be time adjustable if there is an alternative that does not rely on a timer." Our position: the timed session is the essential mechanic of the product, and every informational need has an untimed alternative (open safety tier, public screen, open data and open source). Declared in an izjava o pristupačnosti. Zakon NN 17/19 binds public bodies, not grantees; the EAA law NN 89/2025 exempts micro service providers.
- Cookies: ZEK NN 76/22 art. 43(4) plus AZOP: strictly necessary session cookies (authentication, session) need no consent; analytics cookies do. Design: one first-party session cookie or token for the pairing session, no analytics cookies, no localStorage identifiers for analytics; counters without identifiers (Cloudflare Web Analytics model, psdlat MetricsDO shape). Cloudflare processes metadata in the US and Europe under SCCs.
- Otvorena dozvola (NN 67/17): state the source and date of last change as designated by the body, or "Sadrži informacije tijela javne vlasti u skladu s Otvorenom dozvolom" with a link; mark adaptations; no implied official endorsement. data.zagreb.hr adds "omogući dijeljenje pod sličnim uvjetima", satisfied by publishing our derived data openly.
- Video surveillance of public space is reserved to public bodies (ZPOUZP art. 32); we operate no cameras and embed only permitted feeds.
- De minimis: Regulation 2023/2831, 300,000 EUR over three years per single undertaking; central register from 1 Jan 2026.

## Recommended approach: "Vidikovac" (working name; final name is Matija's call)

Three surfaces from one codebase: Ruka (phone in hand, unlocked), Prozor (public screen, no touch, always readable), Stol (desktop, unlocked like a screen). Seven layers: Grad sada, U pokretu, Zrak i nebo, Sigurnost (open), Uprava i pravo, Kultura i sjećanje, Vijesti. Ten minutes from a screen, five from a person. Everything exportable and shareable; a shared link opens a static attributed "pogled izvana" page plus a map of screens. Alternatives considered and rejected: a lean single-DO gate without mirroring or sharing (cheaper to build, weaker on innovation and social benefit); a platform-first build of every data source before the mechanic (more panels, weaker demo, deadline risk).

All four design sections below were presented to Matija on 11 Sept 2026 and approved as described.

### 1. The mechanic (approved)

Roles and objects:
- Screen (kiosk, or a desktop acting as one): provisioned once on an Access-gated admin route (two-gate pattern from `D:\scratch\psdlat\worker\src\stats.ts`: Access in front, in-Worker JWT check, fail-closed 404). Record `{beaconId (8 base32), secretHash, venueType ∈ kafic|knjiznica|cetvrt|udruga|zet|ostalo, area ∈ 17 gradske četvrti, operatorLabel}`. One-time URL `/kiosk#<beaconId>.<secret>` stores the secret in the kiosk browser's localStorage. Kiosk connects to `/ws/beacon/<beaconId>`; BeaconDO challenges with a nonce (psdlat `mesh-do.ts` lines 476-497 idiom), kiosk answers HMAC(secret, nonce), three failures close the socket. Revocation flag closes the socket and voids codes.
- BeaconDO (one per screen, SQLite): mints codes in batches of 20 slots of 30 s (10 minutes) when a socket authenticates, stores `(code, slotStart, slotEnd, used)`, registers the batch with IndexDO in one call, sends the batch plus `serverNow` to the kiosk; the kiosk rotates on wall-clock boundaries and requests the next batch when three slots remain. No per-slot alarm. The kiosk socket attachment holds `{netKey}` only.
- RoomDO (one per session, SQLite, hibernation): opened by BeaconDO on a successful kiosk redeem with `expiresAt = now + 10 min`, `beaconType`, `area`, two single-use tickets. Sockets join with `{t:'join', ticket}` and receive `{t:'joined', role, expiresAt, serverNow, resumeToken, dataToken}`. Messages: `view {layer, params}` (forwarded only from the driver to kiosk sockets, never phone to phone), `share` (mints peer codes, see below), `codes`, `count`, `event {name, dim}` (analytics, max 60 per socket), `expiring {secondsLeft}` at 60 and 20 s, `expired` then close code 4000 `session-expired`. Alarm chain idempotent on `phase`: live → warned60 → warned20 → closed (broadcast, close all sockets, record `session_end`, `storage.deleteAll()`). Ping/pong via `setWebSocketAutoResponse`.
- IndexDO (singleton): `code → {kind:'kiosk', beaconId} | {kind:'room', roomId}` with expiry; purges every 5 minutes. Needed for the typed-code path; one registration per batch, one lookup per scan.
- MetricsDO (singleton): psdlat `metrics-do.ts` with one added `hour` column.

Code and QR: 8 Crockford base32 characters displayed `ABCD-EFGH` (alphabet `0-9 A-Z` without I, L, O, U; `byte % 32`, no bias; normalisation maps I/L→1, O→0), 40 bits from `crypto.getRandomValues`. Redeemable from slotStart − 5 s to slotEnd + 30 s, single use, flipped atomically inside the owning DO. QR payload `https://zagreb.aningfirm.hr/s#ABCD-EFGH` with the code in the URL fragment (never sent to servers or logs); rendered with psdlat `createQr` (uqr inline SVG, `currentColor`, text fallback). Primary scanner: the phone's native camera app. Secondary: psdlat `qrScanner.ts` (BarcodeDetector, `createDecoder` seam; WASM fallback nimiq/qr-scanner after the deadline). A typed-code field is always present.

Scan: `POST /api/scan {code}` (body cap 64 B). Worker computes `netKey = base64url(HMAC-SHA256(NET_KEY_SECRET, asn + '|' + addr)).slice(0,22)` where addr is the IPv4 address or the first 64 bits of IPv6, from `request.cf.asn` and `CF-Connecting-IP`; raw values discarded. IndexDO resolves the code. Kiosk case: BeaconDO checks window, used, revoked; rejects `same-network` only when scanner netKey equals the kiosk socket's netKey (same ASN and same address); message: "Ovaj zaslon i tvoj telefon dijele istu mrežu. Isključi Wi-Fi i skeniraj mobilnim podacima." Runtime var `NETWORK_CHECK` ∈ enforce|warn|off (enforce in production). Then opens the RoomDO and pushes `{t:'unlocked', roomId, ticket}` to the kiosk. Response to the phone `{roomId, ticket, beaconType, area, expiresAt, participants}`; the phone shows a confirm card ("Zaslon: kafić, Donji grad, 10 minuta" or "Telefon druge osobe, 5 minuta") before joining, which is the possession check and the QRLJacking defence. Peer case: a phone in a room taps "Podijeli grad"; RoomDO mints codes registered as `kind:'room'`; the joiner gets a new room of 5 fresh minutes, one hop only (a peer-granted room cannot share), no network check, no bonus to the giver.

Tokens: `dataToken = base64url(roomId || expiresAt || HMAC-SHA256(SESSION_SECRET, roomId||expiresAt))`, verified statelessly on `/api/data/*`, so polling never wakes a DO; `resumeToken` (16 random bytes, one live socket per token) kept in sessionStorage for reload within the session; no cookies.

Expiry: the phone view freezes as a static, attributed snapshot; live updates and navigation stop, exports keep working; line "Sesija je završila. Prikaz je zamrznut. Zaslon u blizini otključava novih deset minuta." The kiosk returns to teaser and QR. Re-scanning the same screen reopens the same layer ("Nastavi gdje si stao"). No cooldown.

Limits: `RL_SCAN` 10/60 s by IP, `RL_DATA` 240/60 s by dataToken, `RL_OPEN` 120/60 s by IP for open routes (kmeu `wrangler.toml` binding shape, `binding.limit({key})`); BeaconDO slow-down for 60 s after 20 failed redeems; counted sessions capped at 30/hour and 200/day per screen, overflow recorded as `over_cap` and excluded from the City dataset. Turnstile behind flag `SCAN_TURNSTILE`, default off. Body caps, `run_worker_first` so assets never hit the Worker.
