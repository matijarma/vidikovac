# Controller rulings, Vidikovac stage 1 (11 September 2026)

Every decision the build controller took on the owner's behalf while executing the implementation plan, in the order made. Each ruling names the plan text or finding it overrides. Cost if wrong, in general: a ruling that changed a contract (R-03, R-08, R-14, R-22, R-29, R-32, R-42, R-44, R-45, R-50, R-52) would surface as a mismatch between two areas and is defended by the integration and parity tests; a ruling that dropped or reshaped a plan requirement (R-05, R-06, R-11, R-25, R-30, R-41, R-48, R-58, R-61) costs at most a rework of that requirement; process rulings (R-01, R-07, R-09, R-12, R-13, R-40, R-47) cost nothing in product terms. The full ledger of task outcomes lived in the git-ignored SDD workspace and is summarised in docs/HANDOFF-2026-09-11.md.

Made 11 Sept 2026 after the pre-flight conflict scans of the plan. Each ruling overrides the plan text it names. The spec (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-vidikovac-design.md) stays the authority above both.

## Rulings from the A, B, C pre-flight scans (11 Sept 2026, afternoon)

R-22 FeedItem.data keys are a contract between Area A (producer) and Area C (consumer). vehicle: routeId, tripId, vehicleId, routeShortName, bearing, speed (numbers optional). route delay summary item (kind 'vehicle', id 'route:<routeId>'): routeId, routeShortName, medianDelaySeconds, vehicles. closure: type, subtype, direction, street. observation: temp, humidity, pressure, windDir, windSpeed, weather. forecast: tmin, tmax, weather, text. quake: mag, depth, magType, region. warning: event, certainty, urgency. news: source. act: broj, godina, category. poi: layer, category. R-23 A1 creates worker/feed/http.ts, worker/feed/xml.ts AND worker/feed/time.ts (R-01's enumeration was incomplete). isoOrUndefined treats an offset-less timestamp (e.g. CKAN metadata_modified "2026-09-01T08:00:00.000000") as UTC by appending "Z" before parsing; tests assert the UTC result and never depend on the host timezone.
R-24 R-17 clarified: a single, typed event literal at a recordMetric call site (recordMetric(env, 'source_fetch', id, 'ok')) is fine; what R-17 forbids is a second list or allowlist of event names.
