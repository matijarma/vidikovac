### 0.4 Where this file departs from Appendix A, and why

1. Cancellations from alerts with the fallback computed in the twin, which publishes a list; no client re-derives carriers (the stop sheet's timetable call passes no vehicles and would drop the alert trip that ran).
2. **Locality**: a promet notice shows only where a line it names serves the place's boards; novosti items show everywhere. Of 27 distinct promet titles in the fixtures, 20 name a line in the title and two more in the summary; the other five name a terminal, a stop, the funicular or the cable car.
3. **Window 96 h**, not 48 h: the CRO Race notices were published Thursday 07:00 and 12:50 for Sunday, 72 to 77 hours ahead; 48 h hides both on the day (F11). **A weekday in the title** ("u subotu") ends the notice after that day, because ZET removes items up to a day late (line 137's Saturday notice stood all Sunday).
4. **"ZET javlja" in the time cell**, as the timeless row prints "uvijek", not a lead line: a notice has no moment, and its publish time in the time column would read as an event then.
5. **Summary of whole sentences up to 240 characters**, which needs the external-text `summary` cap raised from 180 (`rowMarkup` re-vets every sub): 22 of 27 fixture notices keep a summary at 240, 8 at 180.
6. **Family "ZET javlja: {notice}."**: the slot is `notice` because the copy guard puts every `{title}` inside „…” (`copy-guards.test.ts:525-533`), and the full stop is there because `writeSentence` adds one. It is offered while the row stands, not 24 h: the chooser has no priority by type, and row and header never disagree.
7. The phone row links to ZET's notice page (the plan's credit and link); the wall keeps the credit "ZET javlja".
8. Decoder flags are booleans (`canceled`, `skipped`), not string unions: nothing else is read.

What ZET already says reaches the screens. A trip ZET cancels with a no-service alert leaves the boards on every surface unless a vehicle is driving it; the `CANCELED` marker, which on a normal day marks trips ZET drives, removes nothing. The phone's stop sheet says when ZET cancelled a line's departures. No real departure is removed; no product string names a cause.
