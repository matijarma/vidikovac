## Context

The twin engine (round E, 16 Sept) publishes a forward-extrapolated plan per vehicle every 10 s tick; the client integrator converges a drawn mark onto that plan each frame. Two renderers read the same `Drawn`: the MapLibre city map and the ZET schema canvas. The owner's live tests found six problems. Read-only evidence gathered on 18 Sept (six subsystem readers, two diagnostic lenses, my own reading of the core files) explains each:

## Owner decisions (18 Sept)

- **No fixed display delay.** "I expect us to extrapolate based on all the data and real-life experience to arrive at the best possible result where in most cases if you look on our map and on the actual street you see seemingly real-time tracking; the ones that aren't look to users as GPS lagging, not our app." This fixes the engine's rule for the round: **bias behind, never ahead; catch up forwards; hold when contradicted.** A mark behind the real tram reads as lag; a mark ahead that has to come back reads as a broken app. First the plan is made *correct* (the phantom stops go), then it is tilted late by design, and the client never draws a tram moving backwards.
- **Schema names:** horizontal, crossing the coloured lines at the stop ring, pills painting over the text (owner's idea, confirmed against two alternatives with previews).
- **Terminals:** larger filled disc with a ring, name in bold capitals, small numbered chips of the lines ending there.
- **Direction nose:** only between zoom 14.5 and 16.5.
- **Process:** the owner asked not to spend agents where they add nothing; the remaining verification agents were stopped, and the plan stands on direct code reading plus the two completed diagnostic lenses.
- Assumptions taken without asking (overridable): "only this line" is the default when a line or tram is selected, persisted per device; the per-stop dwell table is readable on `/stats`; the schema pill is the same tram-blue numbered pill as the city map (the line under it and the terminal chips carry the ZET colour); on the schema, focus mode dims other lines instead of hiding them.

## Design

### E0. The served-stop table (foundation for everything on the server)

The network artefact (`app/public/data/zet-network.json`, `scripts/gtfs-shapes.mjs`) goes to **version 3**: each path carries `served: [{ stop, s }]`, the platforms its patterns actually call at, in arc order, derived from `zet-trips.json` patterns (the union of the patterns that run the shape, so short-turn variants are covered; synthetic paths already carry their sequence). Each synthetic path is keyed by its pattern hash so every shapeless variant gets its own path (closes D11), and `scheduleTimes` maps a pattern to its own path by id. Stops gain `terminal: boolean` (first or last stop of any pattern) and `direction` per served link. The geometric `onEdge` links stay for matching and for the city map's stop circles.

`shared/motion/network.ts` decodes v3 (`SUPPORTED_VERSION` 3; the version error keeps a stale cache loud), `graph.ts` `stopsOnPath` returns the served list where present and the geometric list only for a path without one, and every consumer reads it: the planner's `stopsAhead`/`stopAt`, `learn.ts` evidence, `speed.ts` `stopsBetween` (which also receives the per-stop dwell instead of the 20 s constant), `laws`/`order.ts` concession gate, `publish.ts` `held`, the matcher's `atStop` annotation, and the `/stats` dwell table. The schema placer already dedupes by name and is unaffected. Budget pins and the `feedVersion` cross-checks are updated; the artefact size is pinned again once measured.

This alone removes the systematic slowness (D1) that round E papered over with 15 s of own speed, and it is what the owner called "the exact stations list" the order algorithm should reason over.

### A. Pills that never disappear, on both maps

A shared pure module `app/src/motion/pills.ts` (DOM-free, unit-tested) owns the pill's look and the cluster rule:

- **Geometry and inks.** `PILL_HEIGHT_PX`, the width rule and the tram/bus fill and text colours (light and dark) move out of `overlays.ts`/`basemap.ts` into `pills.ts`; the width becomes `pillWidthPx(chars)` and `overlayImages()` generates SDF images for every width a cluster label can take (1 to about 14 characters).
- **Clustering.** `clusterPills(marks)` groups marks whose pill boxes (plus 2 px padding) overlap in screen space, by union-find, into one cluster mark per group: label = distinct line numbers sorted numerically joined with a middle dot ("6·11·12"), capped at four numbers then "+n"; `ids` of the members; centred on the members' centroid; drawn as one pill with a 2 px halo ring so it reads as "several here". The selected or followed vehicle is never absorbed.
- **City map.** The pill layer gets `icon-allow-overlap`, `text-allow-overlap`, `icon-ignore-placement`, `text-ignore-placement` true: never dropped, never blocking a stop name (this also retires the stop-label ordering problem). Clustering runs in `city-map.ts` at each 12 Hz push with `map.project`; cluster features carry `kind: 'cluster'`, `short`, `ids`, `n`. A tap on a cluster below zoom 17.5 eases the camera in on its members; at or above, it selects the nearest member. `test/motion/city-map.test.ts:385` and `test/app/overlays.test.ts:51-53` change accordingly.
- **Schema.** `VehicleMark` gains `label`, `pill: 'single' | 'cluster'`, `ids`; a new `paintPills` in `schema-paint.ts` draws capsule + centred text (12 px × density × `symbolScale`, app font stack, opaque, halo); the lagano crop scene keeps `paintVehicles` untouched. Marks are clipped at the canvas edge with a pill-sized margin instead of culled, and a selection stays alive while the vehicle exists in `Drawn` even if momentarily unplaceable (no `closeCard('gone')` for an out-of-frame mark). The accessible list stays one button per vehicle; a cluster hit behaves as on the city map.

### B. Schema legibility: names and terminals

`schema-paint.ts` static layer; the artwork's `rot`/`anchor` no longer drive placement, the artefact is unchanged:

- **Names** horizontal, `textAlign: center`, centred on the stop's canonical ring, drawn over the lines: a translucent halo stroke in the canvas tone (alpha 0.75, 3 px) then ink text at weight 600, size `max(7.92 units × scale, 11 px)` capped at 16 px; two-row labels keep their rows. Paint order: water, lines, names, rings; the vehicle canvas above, so pills drive over the text.
- **Collision**: greedy by rank (terminals, then stops by lines calling, then name) against labels already placed at this scale; a colliding name is skipped and appears on zoom-in. This replaces the all-or-nothing threshold on interactive maps; the kiosk crop keeps its 24 px floor and all names.
- **Terminals**: disc radius 1.6× the ordinary ring, ink fill with a paper gap ring; name in 700 CAPS at 9.4 units; under it a row of chips, one per schema line whose first or last `stops[]` entry is this stop, each a small rounded rect in the line's colour with the number in contrasting text (`app/src/ui/contrast.ts`).

### C. Line focus and a lighter network

- **Default weight** (non-kiosk): tram `zoomInterpolate(10, 0.6, 13, 1.1, 16, 2.4)`, bus `zoomInterpolate(10, 0.45, 13, 0.85, 16, 1.9)`; opacity unchanged; the kiosk figure keeps its ruling.
- **ZET colours for the city map**: `scripts/zet-schema.mjs` also writes `app/src/data/zet-line-colours.json` (`{ routeId: '#rrggbb' }`, 19 lines, deterministic, `feedVersion`-checked); `pills.ts` exposes `lineColour(routeId, fallback)`.
- **Focus mode**: new `lineFocus: boolean` in `OverlayOptions`/`CityMapOptions`, handle method `setLineFocus(on)` (a new name; `setEmphasis` already means city-point kinds). The focused route is the selected route or the selected/followed vehicle's `routeId` (resolved from `lastDrawn`). Focus on: network layers filtered to the focused route (hidden, not dimmed), `network-selected` painted in `lineColour(routeId)` with the halo casing, every other pill still drawn (the inverted ink for other routes stays). Focus off: today's behaviour with the selected route in its ZET colour. The schema dims non-focused lines to alpha 0.2 and draws the focused line last.
- **The toggle**: a `role="switch"` row in both `routeDetailMarkup` and `vehicleDetailMarkup` (`app/src/transport/view.ts`), `data-action="toggle-line-focus"`, labels `transport.lineFocusOn` ("Samo ova linija na karti") / `transport.lineFocusOff` ("Cijela mreža na karti"), handled in the workspace's delegated switch, persisted in `app/src/core/line-focus-store.ts` (`kajima:line-focus:v1`, default `true`, the `map-mode-store.ts` pattern) and passed through `LayerContext`. The kiosk keeps its own contract.

### D. Direction nose

`overlays.ts`: `NOSE_MIN_ZOOM = 14.5`, `NOSE_MAX_ZOOM = 16.5` as the nose layers' `maxzoom`, reason in the comment: from 16.5 the rails draw ≥ 4 px apart and the tram's own rail says the direction. The selected vehicle's nose follows the same band.

### E. Motion: bias behind, never reverse, never overtake

#### E1. Client integrator (`app/src/motion/integrator.ts`)

- **No backward motion, any kind**: `convergeArc` and `convergePoint` clamp the step to non-negative along the path or toward the target's forward direction; a target behind the mark is a **hold** (`Drawn.holding: true`, unused visually this round) until the plan catches up. `BACKWARD_MAX_MS`/`BACKWARD_BUDGET_M` go. A vehicle whose type resolves to −1 but whose plan runs a rail path is a tram.
- **Edge-aware sticky clamp fed by the wire**: `edgeAt`/`mapArc` move to `shared/motion/order.ts` and the client uses them; pairs are any two trams whose current edge lies on the other's path; the relation comes from the twin's published leader (`Fix.behind`) and only falls back to plan order for unrelated vehicles; roles change only when the wire says so. The ceiling is `min(leader.s, leader.targetS) − HEADWAY_M` and is applied *through* convergence as an upper bound (no direct write): a follower that would have to move back instead holds.
- **Re-seed on geometry change**: exact `mapArc` when the new path runs the current edge, else a windowed projection around the mapped arc; never a global nearest point.
- **Reduced motion**: the once-a-second tick integrates the full elapsed second (cap 1 s), so a mark keeps pace with its plan.
- `test/motion/integrator.test.ts:152-190` changes to assert zero backward frames and a hold, cross-path order on the corridor's shared trunk, and no role flip without the wire.

#### E2. Twin planner: correct first, then late by design (`plan.ts`, `times.ts`, `learn.ts`, `speed.ts`, new `shared/motion/dwell.ts`)

- **Per-stop dwell table** (`dwell.ts`): for every served platform a `default` (the timetable median where > 0, else `DWELL_DEFAULT_S` 20; the table is where a per-class default can later live) and a `dynamic` estimate: the `DWELL_PLAN_QUANTILE = 0.7` quantile of the last `DWELL_RECENT_N = 30` samples inside `DWELL_RECENT_WINDOW_S = 5400` (90 min) when at least `DWELL_RECENT_MIN = 5` exist, else the banded histogram's 0.7 quantile where the cell (with borrowing) holds ≥ 10 samples, else the default. Recent samples ride in `TwinState.dwellRecent` and flush to SQLite `stop_dwell_recent` beside `stop_dwell`. Dwell evidence gains a **stationarity check** (≥ 2 fixes within `DEAD_ZONE_M` along the arc inside the zone) and prices "other stops" from the same table (closes D10 and the learner poisoning in D1).
- **Planner state fixes** (D8): an anchor inside a stop zone is a stand unless a later fix proves departure (arc beyond the zone by more than the dead zone); `standing` is decided along the arc with a scatter-sized tolerance (`STAND_SCATTER_M = 30`); the first stretch never runs at a cruise measured before the last stop (an interval with `ds` under the dead zone is evidence of speed ≈ 0 and pulls the estimate down); the "departed by the header" bound from a TripUpdate applies only when the named stop is at least two ahead or the update's own time post-dates a moving fix.
- **Quantiles, not medians**: `TimesProvider` answers at a quantile; the planner books stretches at `PLAN_QUANTILE = 0.65` of the learned edge histograms (median where thin) and dwells from the table's 0.7 quantile, so a correct plan is tilted late. Own speed still rules the first `OWN_SPEED_HOLD_S`.
- **Junction waits**: `learn.ts` mines stationary runs off any served platform within `JUNCTION_ZONE_M = 60` before a node of degree > 2 (degree from `edges[].from/to`) into `node_wait` histograms per (node, hour band, day type) with a pass count, so `p(stop)` and the wait distribution are known; the planner inserts the 0.5 quantile wait when `p(stop) ≥ 0.4`. SQLite table `node_wait`.
- **Published floor**: `buildPlan` receives the previous published plan's arc at the new header; an anchor behind it by less than `ANCHOR_NOISE_M = 25` starts from the published arc (noise, not a reversal); beyond that the honest plan is published and the client holds (closes D3 for noise).

#### E3. Ordering register, matcher, identity (`shared/motion/order.ts` replaces `laws.ts`; `match.ts`; `tick.ts`)

- **Register**: per vehicle `leader: id | null` and per relation the evidence counters, keyed to the **shared stretch** (the run of edges both paths traverse from the pair's current position), not the leader's current edge, so junction micro-edges do not drop it (closes D7). Establishment uses **fixes, not plans**: fixes separated by more than `ORDER_ESTABLISH_M = 60` along the shared stretch with the same sign on two consecutive fresh fixes, or ZET's TripUpdates naming served stops in strict sequence on the same path (A's next stop precedes B's), the deterministic witness the owner asked for; the previous tick's published order breaks ties. Once written the relation is **sticky** through bunching (closes D5) and ends only when both have left the shared stretch, when the follower's fix leads the leader's fix by more than `SWAP_LIMIT_M` 300 m (stale), or by **concession**: `CONCESSION_FIXES = 3` consecutive fresh follower fixes ahead of the leader's fix by more than `ORDER_ESTABLISH_M`, with the leader within 40 m of a *served* stop or the path end (a follower whose fix has left the shared stretch ahead of the leader's fix is decisive at once). Contradictions count per fresh fix.
- **Enforcement** sorts each shared stretch once by the register and sweeps leader-first (order-independent, no fixpoint needed): a **hold** clamps the follower's knots to `leader − HEADWAY_M`, never below its own fix; a **push** raises only the leader's knots at times at or after the follower's fix time (the follower's fix is a lower bound on where the leader was *from then on*, not before), never its anchor (closes D6's teleport). Buses stay exempt.
- **Wire**: `data.behind: <vehicleId>` joins `DATA_KEYS` (`worker/feed/schema.ts:158`, fixture test) and reaches the client as `Fix.behind`; the order report is emitted as metric `twin_order` (pushes, holds, concessions, relations).
- **Matcher** (`match.ts`): direction-aware re-derivation, when two consecutive fixes move against the path tangent by more than `FOLD_MOVE_M` and a directed edge within `NEAR_M` agrees with the movement, re-derive to that edge's path of the same route and opposite direction (closes D4, and a terminus turnaround is read as a turnaround); `adoptPath` prefers the agreeing path; loops use the occurrence nearest the previous arc.
- **Identity across trips** (`tick.ts`): a trip change keeps the `Track` when the new trip's prior path runs the vehicle's current edge or starts within `STOP_ZONE_M` of the vehicle; only path-derived match state resets; relations prune by the normal divergence rule. A vehicle is followed depot to depot under its vehicle id (closes D14). The replay day answers whether ZET vehicle ids are stable through the day before this lands.

#### E4. Measurement, first and last

- **Signed hindsight**: metric `twin_hindsight_sign` (dim1 horizon, dim2 `ahead_ge50 | within50 | behind_ge50`) beside the existing buckets; `/stats` shows it. Round target at 30 s: `ahead_ge50` ≤ 10 % of graded fixes, with `behind_ge50` not worse than today's overall ≥ 50 m share and p95 not worse than the baseline.
- **Replay grader** (`scripts/replay-core.ts`): between-plan regressions (published position at the new header behind the previous plan at the same time by > 25 m); fix-order violations (two trams on a shared stretch with fixes within 5 s whose published order disagrees with the fixes); phantom-stop count per path (served vs geometric, to prove E0); and a **client simulation**: the integrator over the replayed payloads with a client clock polling at header + 3.5 s and stepping at 12 Hz, counting visible backward frames (must be 0), visible crossings on shared stretches (must be 0), hold-time share and mean hold length. The table gains these rows; `docs/kaj-verification.md` gets the definitions.
- **Baseline**: pull one full recorded day from R2 (`vidikovac-feed/zet-rt/2026/09/17/`, `--remote`, the loop in the script header) before any engine change; record the table; every engine task re-runs the same day. The 16 Sept recordings expire around 23 Sept, so the pull happens first.

### F. Stats surface

`/stats` (`worker/stats/page.ts`): per-stop dwell table (stop, default, dynamic p50/p70, recent samples, last sample time), junction-wait table, `twin_order` by kind, signed hindsight.

## Risks

- **Bias behind vs "seemingly real time"**: a plan that errs late makes the mark reach a stop a few seconds after the tram; the quantiles are the dial and F11 tunes them on the replay day against the signed hindsight. The owner's rule decides ties: late reads as lag, early reads as broken.
- **Artefact v3** touches every decoder and the budget pin; the version error keeps a cached v2 from being misread, and the schema artefact's `feedVersion` is unaffected because the GTFS feed does not change.
- **Cluster pills at dense hubs**: five or six numbers collapse to four plus "+n"; a tap zooms in. Fixed SDF widths avoid `icon-text-fit` uncertainties.
- **Horizontal names crossing lines** in dense corridors can still collide; the rank-greedy pass hides the lowest-rank names until zoom, the same behaviour the city map's stop labels have.
- **Register hysteresis** (60 m to establish, three fixes to swap) delays a genuine swap by up to three fixes (~30–45 s) and only where physics allows it.
- **Identity across trips** relies on ZET vehicle ids being stable through the day; the replay day answers this before F10 lands, and the fallback is today's per-trip track.
- **Wire change** (`behind`) is additive; old clients ignore it.
