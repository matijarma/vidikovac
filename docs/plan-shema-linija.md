# Part 2: the ZET schema mode (16 Sept 2026, after round E shipped)

## Context

The twin engine is live (round E deployed 06:39 UTC, `main` `a24266f`). Matija tested it, is satisfied, and asked for the next feature: a **"ZET tram schema" map mode**. People should be able to toggle the live transit map between the OpenStreetMap-based city map and the classic ZET tram network diagram everyone knows from the stops, with the live trams moving on it. The source artwork is ZET's own map, already in the repo: `zet-zagreb-tram-lines-map.svg` (repo root, committed 15 Sept in the owner's auto-commit `60b0c0d`, 1,163,805 bytes, an A3 PDF export, viewBox `0 0 1190.5512 841.8898`). It must lose its header, legend, logo, footer note and the line-number badges that visually break the lines; those are not needed live.

Owner decisions taken 16 Sept (plan-mode questions):

- **S1 Re-render from extracted geometry**, not the shipped SVG: a build script decodes the artwork into a small JSON artefact and the app draws it on canvas (theme-aware, crisp labels in the app font, tens of KB). ZET's exact glyph shapes are not reproduced.
- **S2 Stop names stay**, shown when zoomed in (hidden at fit-to-screen on a phone, shown as the person zooms in, always on the kiosk and a wide desk).
- **S3 The toggle goes everywhere the live map appears**: the transport workspace in the dashboard (desk, landscape and phone stages, including the phone session) and the kiosk field map.
- **Buses are out of this feature and out of further engine work for now** ("not expected to be as punctual as trams"): the schema shows trams only, and a handoff note records the parked bus work (timetable for bus shapes) for a future round.
- Lightweight ("lagano") mode is unchanged: no schema there, no toggle shown.

Quality over speed is the only constraint; tests written lean (R-TE19), every suite and gate run in full.

## Decisions (mine, overridable)

1. **Two files, one truth.** `scripts/zet-schema.mjs` (plain Node, `fast-xml-parser`) reads the SVG and writes `app/public/data/zet-schema.json` (committed, regenerable with `npm run build:schema`, deterministic: sorted keys, coordinates rounded to 0.01 units, so the artefact diffs cleanly); the SVG stays in the repo as the source, never served. Excluded regions (header, legend rectangle, footer notice, the twelve images and `#im3`) are explicit rectangles in the overrides file, and the script prints whatever unassigned artwork remains inside the diagram box. The script refuses to run when `network-meta.ts`'s `FEED_VERSION` differs from the network artefact it validates against, prints a report (strokes with their route, termini and badge votes; circles per line, projected stops without an own circle; matched, overridden, allowlisted and unmatched names) and exits non-zero on any unresolved line, any unmatched tram name outside the allowlist, or any tram path whose stop order is not monotone on its line (decision 3).
2. **Line identity by termini and badges, as a bijection** (see findings), overrides in `scripts/zet-schema-overrides.json` keyed by GTFS route id → stroke (by colour and, where two share one, by a point on it) plus name aliases and the `unmapped` allowlist. Night lines are extracted too (routes 31 to 34 in GTFS, the four dark-blue strokes) so a night tram rides its own line.
3. **Placement by stop bracketing, validated at build.** Stops in the artefact are keyed by **stop name as the network carries it** (platform ids shift with feeds; `stopsOnPath()` entries carry `stop.name`). A stroke's stop list is: stops whose own circle lies within 2.5 units of it, plus stops whose circle group (connected components of circles within 8 units) lies within the corridor width, projected onto the stroke (the artwork draws one circle per corridor and lets the label span the parallel lines). At runtime, a tram on path P at arc `s`: the schema line L of P's route; the path's ordered stops `stopsOnPath(P)` are matched to L's stops by name and indexed by position `k` (never by name, so the circuit patterns of lines 9 and 17 whose stops appear twice are handled); the two matched stops bracketing `s` (k⁻, k⁺) give `u` by linear interpolation and the point is L at `u`; **before the first and after the last matched stop the arc clamps**, so a tram waiting at Črnomerec sits on its terminal dot rather than vanishing. When k⁻ and k⁺ are not adjacent on L (a detour or short-turn pattern serving stops the stroke passes between), the tram runs the straight chord between the two circles and carries no `track`: drawn, honest, never gliding through stops it does not serve. Heading sign comes from `du/ds` of the bracket, not from `direction_id`; `track` is L's tangent at `u`; `heading` is set only when the integrator's own heading is known (decision 5 of Part 1). **No lateral offset by direction** (invisible at fit, a false position when zoomed; opposite trams overlapping for a moment is true). A path with fewer than two matched stops on L, a bus, a free-plane vehicle or an off-graph tram is **not drawn**; the legend says "tramvaji na svojim linijama" so the omission is honest. The build cross-validates: for every tram path in the network artefact (all shapes and the 45 synthetic paths) the `u` sequence of its matched stops must be strictly monotone, and coverage per path is reported.
4. **Labels** are canvas text in the app's Manrope at the artwork's anchor and rotation (multi-row ones as two rows), drawn when the schema's scale exceeds `LABEL_MIN_PX_PER_UNIT` (about 1.4, where a 7.92 pt label reaches 11 CSS px; the constant with its reason in the file); termini larger. Line colours are ZET's from the artefact in both themes (they are the identity of the lines); everything else takes `--tone-*` tokens: canvas from `--tone-surface-canvas`, circles filled with the canvas tone and stroked with `--tone-text-primary`, labels `--tone-text-primary`, water a muted tint chosen from `tokens.css` at implementation (no new colour literals in CSS).
5. **Viewport**: `app/src/ui/pan-zoom.ts` over Pointer Events after the sheet's capture pattern: one-finger drag, two-pointer pinch around the midpoint, wheel zoom around the cursor, double-tap zoom-in, `touch-action: none` on the canvas, no inertia, scale clamped to [fit, 8 × fit], translation clamped so the diagram never leaves the viewport; keyboard `+`, `-`, `0` zoom and Shift+arrows pan (plain arrows stay with vehicle selection). Fit-to-canvas on mount and on "fit-city", honouring the sheet detent through `fitPadding()` exactly as MapLibre does. Selection of a vehicle or a stop centres on it at a legible scale. Reduced motion: no animated fits. **Kiosk**: non-interactive; because the whole network at a 1080p fit leaves labels at about 10 px (under the kiosk's 24 px floor), the kiosk shows a **crop around the screen's stop** at a scale where labels are legible (five to six stops each way, the same shape as the kiosk's geographic view, R-P1), labels always on inside it, and falls back to the whole network without labels when the screen has no stop. Overridable by the owner.
6. **Vehicle marks** reuse `paintVehicles`/`hitVehicle` (their layout parameter loosened to `{ w, h, density }`): tram glyphs oriented by `track`, tinted with the line colour so a tram reads as its line even when the label is hidden; alpha from confidence as today. On the dashboard a tap calls `onSelect({ kind: 'vehicle' | 'stop' })` so the transport sheet stays the detail surface (no dialog card); the kiosk is non-interactive.
7. **Slot swap** (see findings): `renderer` on `CityMapOptions`/`MapSlotOptions`; the page factories in `entries/dashboard.ts` and `entries/kiosk.ts` branch to `createSchemaMap(options)` from `app/src/motion/schema-map.ts`, which implements the handle: `update` (through `pointsToFixes`, city-map.ts:233), `pause/resume/destroy`, `setTheme`, `setLocale`, `select/selection`, `follow/following`, `resize`, `setModes` (tolerates a buses-only set with an honest empty state), `setFeedState` (holds on `down` only, R-TE5), `setStop` (highlights the screen's stop), `fit`, `setFitPadding`, `status`, `network`, `vehicles()` (lon/lat from the integrator's metre plane, for the sheet lists); `setOutline`, `setEmphasis`, `setView` are no-ops. `onUserMove` is widened to `(camera: MapCamera | null)` and the schema calls it with `null` on a user pan so `following` clears without inventing a geographic camera. The workspace uses slot id `u-pokretu-schema` for the schema. MapLibre is never loaded in schema mode.
8. **Preference**: `kajima:map-mode:v1` (`'map' | 'schema'`, default `'map'`), a store in `app/src/core/map-mode-store.ts`, per device; the kiosk reads `?prikaz=shema|karta` first, then the store, once at boot.
9. **The toggle**: one button in `.t-map-tools` with `data-action="toggle-map-mode"`, `aria-pressed` when the schema is on, label from `transport.mapModeSchema` ("Shema linija") / `transport.mapModeMap` ("Karta grada"), an icon added to `ICON_NAMES` (a `route`-like Lucide path), `data-testid="map-mode-toggle"`. In schema mode the bus chip is hidden and `transport.schemaTramsOnly` ("Shema prikazuje tramvaje") stands in the legend; the tram chip stays.
10. **Honesty note** (`motion.note`) is rendered under the schema as under the lightweight schematic.
11. **Provenance** goes into `docs/izvori.md` as a row of its own: the artwork is ZET's (the map at the stops), the app re-renders its geometry and names, the basis stated as the file states it for the GTFS artefacts. Whether to tell ZET (as with the GTFS letter, M2) is an **owner item**, not decided here.
12. **Sharing the accessible vehicle list**: `schematic-view.ts`'s a11y list, keyboard selection and idle logic (lines 363-652) are parameterised by a `Scene` interface (`resize(w, h, density)`, `marks(drawn): VehicleMark[]`, `inFrame(v)`, `paintStatic(ctx, tones)`, `onChange(cb)` for pan and zoom repaints, `listCap`) with an `onSelect` hook that replaces the dialog card; the existing lightweight crop scene is the first implementation and its tests stay green; the schema scene is the second. Duplicating the accessibility contract would be the worse outcome.

## The artefact: `app/public/data/zet-schema.json`

```
{ version: 1, source: 'zet-zagreb-tram-lines-map.svg', builtAt, feedVersion (of the network artefact it was validated against),
  box: [1190.5512, 841.8898],                      // artwork units, y down (page space, all transforms composed)
  lines: [{ route: '6', night: false, colour: '#cc706f', width: 3.5345,
            pts: [[x, y], ...],                   // the artwork's own M/L vertices, rounded to 0.01
            stops: [{ u, name, ownCircle: bool }] }],   // u = arc along pts, ascending; name = the network's stop name
  stops: [{ name, x, y, r, half: null | 'D', label: { text, rows: 1|2, x, y, rot, anchor: 'start'|'end' } | null, terminal: bool }],
  water: [{ pts, width, colour }] }
```

Target: under 60 KB raw / 20 KB gzip (pinned in `test/scripts/zet-schema.test.ts` with headroom, by ruling once measured).

## Risks

- **Artwork matching residue**: a few labels or circles may resist automatic association (dense hubs like Trg bana Jelačića, Glavni kolodvor with several parallel lines). The override table and the loud report make each residue a named decision, not a silent gap; the merge waits for zero unresolved.
- **Paths not on the schema**: trams re-derived onto other lines' rails and off-graph trams are hidden by rule 3; detour and short-turn patterns run the chord between the stops they do serve. The legend states trams-on-lines; the count of hidden trams is not shown (a number would raise more questions than it answers).
- **Canvas gestures on phones**: pointer-event pinch is hand-written (no library); tested in a unit scenario, on the mobile Playwright project and by eye. Fallback: the fit button always recovers.
- **Kiosk chapters**: the schema ignores chapter cameras and outlines; a kiosk pinned to the schema shows the crop around its stop through all chapters by design (decision 5), documented in `docs/kiosk.md`. A screen without a stop sees the whole network without labels.
- **A GTFS rebuild without a schema rebuild**: the `feedVersion` cross-check in the artefact test fails CI until `npm run build:schema` is re-run; the runbook in `docs/izvori.md` lists it beside `build:network` and `build:trips`.
- **Provenance**: ZET's artwork is theirs; the app re-renders geometry and names. Owner item (see decision 11).
