### 0.4 Where this file departs from Appendix B, and why

1. **The artefact changes** (decision 29): the builder counted a block's midday pull-in as service, so a normal weekday read 0.67 to 0.76 at 09:00 to 13:00, a midday `reduced` could not return, and Sunday 27 Sep read 248 false minutes; with the split and the look-ahead, 0 false minutes on all six days and a lowest ratio held five minutes of 0.75 (11:00 weekday 462 → 384, 07:00 462 → 460).
2. **Per-mode entry** (decision 30): city-only missed a tram stoppage at the 07:30 peak (city ratio 0.50); with the rule, `reduced` in 5.6 min, 0 false minutes.
3. **N = 40 min**, not 20: the timetable expects fewer than 20 vehicles until 03:55 on a weekday; in production the state was already `silent` from 00:54:50 and held (strike-ratio.md).
4. **Timetable wording** (decision 32): a normal day shows 0.79 to 0.92 of the scheduled runs, so "uobičajeno oko" would overstate by 1.1 to 1.25.
5. **Judged at frames, at the header time**: a tick without a new frame only applies the stale hold, so a gap under 180 s neither ages the count nor flips a state, and a replay of frames judges exactly as production. No `--tick-through` flag is needed; judged frames more than 180 s apart break every dwell, as a hold would.
6. **`routes` lists routes with one trip or more** (not three), so 0 of 2 seen is not confirmed.
7. **`silent` says no departure at all** in the header, live ones included, which matches U4's `silent-departures` row (`/\bpolazi\b/`, trains excepted) and the brief's "no timetable departure is said"; the list keeps a real vehicle's countdown (the one bus of 29 Sep).
8. **No bike radius ×2**: bikes move forward by order only; a station beyond the frame is no alternative a rider walks to.
9. **Cold start is `unknown`**; it reads `normal` at the first judged frame that is not low, and otherwise runs the dwell timers of `normal`.
10. **No metrics event**: a state change is one `logInfo('twin_service', …)` line (the learned writers are cut).

## 1. Goal

The twin compares the vehicles it publishes with the runs the timetable has in service, judges the difference with hysteresis and holds, and publishes `normal`, `reduced`, `silent` or `unknown` beside the source's freshness. The surfaces change voice by state, never by cause: in `reduced` only confirmed lines keep a timetable sentence and the deviation is said once with its numbers; in `silent` no departure is said and one quiet note explains; bikes and rail move forward. It would have caught the strike (F8, F13) and is invisible on a normal day.

Constants: `MIN_EXPECTED = 20` (the night trough runs 00:50 to 03:55 weekdays, to 04:00 Saturdays, to 05:00 Sundays; 30 changes no false minute and delays judging 5 min); `EXPECT_LEAD_S = 600` (5 min leaves Sunday 27 a margin of 0.15, 15 min delays the collapse's `silent` past the night hold); `REDUCED_BELOW = 0.5`, `MODE_REDUCED_BELOW = 0.4`, `REDUCED_AFTER_S = 300` (T1 stays ≥ 3 min: after ZET's 07:15 gaps the header resumes before the positions and the ratio reads 0 for about a minute); `SILENT_SHARE = 0.1`, `SILENT_FLOOR = 2`, `SILENT_AFTER_S = 600`; `LIFT_AT = 0.25`, `LIFT_AFTER_S = 180`; `NORMAL_AT = 0.7`, `MODE_NORMAL_AT = 0.6`, `NORMAL_AFTER_S = 300`; `JUDGE_HEADER_AGE_S = EVICT_S` (decision 18); `CONTINUITY_S = 180`; `ROUTE_BELOW = 0.5`.

`expectationAt(expect, t)`: `expectedAt` at the start of `t`'s slot, with `blocks.all`, `.tram` and `.bus` each lowered to their minimum over the slots at +300 s and +600 s, skipping a later slot that is not `known` (prep-E's `splitLead`); `known`, `reason` and `routes` from the first slot.

Rules, in order, at every tick:
1. No artefact: `unknown`, reason `no-artefact`, timers cleared.
2. No header, or `nowSec − headerTs > 180`: **hold** (state, since and `last` kept, timers cleared).
3. Not a new frame: nothing changes.
4. Judge at `t = headerTs` (ages at the header time, decision 30): `e = expectationAt(expect, t)`; not `known`: `unknown`, reason `no-calendar`, timers cleared. `e.blocks.all < 20`: hold with reason `below-min`, numbers refreshed, timers cleared.
5. If `judgedAtSec` is more than 180 s before `t`, clear the timers first. A mode is judged when its own expected is ≥ 20. Each timer holds the second its condition began: *low* = city ratio < 0.5 or a judged mode's ratio < 0.4; *silent* = seen ≤ max(2, 0.1 × expected); *lift* = ratio ≥ 0.25; *normal* = ratio ≥ 0.7 and every judged mode ≥ 0.6.
6. From `normal` or `unknown`: silent for 600 s → `silent`, else low for 300 s → `reduced`, else `unknown` → `normal` when not low. From `reduced`: silent for 600 s → `silent`, else normal for 300 s → `normal`. From `silent`: lift for 180 s → `reduced` (the normal timer keeps running, so a full return reads `normal` 300 s after it began). A judged frame clears the reason. A change of state, or the first state a fresh memory takes (rules 1 and 4 included, so a cold start at night publishes `unknown` with `below-min`), sets `sinceSec` to that instant.
7. `last`: `expected` (`e.blocks.all`), `seen`, `ratio` (two decimals), `confidence = clamp((expected − 20) / 40, 0, 1) × (nowSec − headerTs ≤ 30 ? 1 : 0.5)`, `baseline: 'declared'`, `byMode` from `track.kind` against `e.blocks.tram/bus`, `observedAt = t`, and in `reduced` and `silent` only `routes`, from `expectedAt(expect, t, allRoutes).routes` against the published tracks per `routeId`.

## 8. Risks

- ZET's midnight defect (vehicles stamped a day ahead) lowers `seen`; the worst normal night kept 0.75 for five minutes. If a night trips anyway, `reduced` holds through the trough and clears by about 04:05.
- A one-mode stoppage reads `reduced`; the city numbers then understate how local it is, and the per-route pairs carry the voice for the missing lines. Per-mode states are the next dimension (brief §8).
- Calendar horizon: 000396 ends 31 Dec; after it the state reads `unknown` (`no-calendar`) and the voice is today's; U0's artefact check fails loudly first.
- Single routes swing (layovers, a route id carried into the next trip): in a `reduced` city a line may lose its timetable sentence wrongly; never a false statement.
- Copy pins in four places move together (catalogue, `SENTENCE_COPY_*`, `SENTENCE_FAMILIES`, `copy-guards`).
