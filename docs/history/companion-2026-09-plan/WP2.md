## Goal

A screen with a place (today: its configured ScreenStop; the setup WP later feeds an address) frames its map on a square of side 2·R(N) centred on the place, N ∈ {4,6,8} "stajališta odavde", default 6, R = {4:1300, 6:2000, 8:2700} m — the same constant the "U blizini · 2 km · ~15 min" circle uses (brief §12; measured p50 air distance to the N-th tram stop in feed 000395: 1519 / 2191 / 2847 m, review.local/companion/plan/WP2/notes.md). The frame is a neighbourhood: buses on, pharmacy address on, ranked stop names plus interchanges, major street names on. On it every BAJS station is a counted disc (teal with its number; grey with "0"; grey without a number when the count is unknown or the station is not renting — never "?"), culture venues appear only with a programme tonight and are named, no geographic "+N" cluster bubbles anywhere, vehicle pills list every line and grow (no "+N" fold), closures unchanged, air stations exist as an option that is off on the wall, legend of three plain items without "?". The empty setup (no place) keeps today's whole-city window. The rules live in shared map modules (map/frame.ts, city/curated.ts, MAP_PRESENTATIONS.placeClusters, CityMapOptions.cityLabels 'venues', ProzorOptions) so WP4's phone Karta reuses them unchanged.
