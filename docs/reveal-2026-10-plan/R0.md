### 0.5 Changes against the plan and the task (with evidence)

Consequences, each a deliberate flip of an older pin (rewritten in Step 4): a second or third departure (100) now outlasts a closure (60) and a sunset (20), which reverses the D5.16/D5.19 rule "a third tram is worth less than a closure" (`timeline.ts:217-235` doc, test `kiosk-timeline.test.ts:633-634`); decision 67's `outlivedBy` (`timeline.ts:276`) is subsumed (a far solar, opening or event row has a lower value than any departure within 2 h); U0 step 7's "an event yields before a closure" becomes a tie (60 each), broken by list position.
