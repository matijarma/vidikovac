2. **The cell drawing.** Measured with Manrope 700 at the dark read tier (44 px): "za 4 min" 159 px, "za 10 min" 181, "12:41" 103, "sada" 86, a two-digit badge 75 (44 px text plus `padding: 0 .35em`, `kiosk.css:349`). So the landscape walls stack (badge and headsign, then the time) and the totem draws the brief's line. Heights: about 100 px on the landscape walls and 64 or about 91 on the totem, not "64, up to 96". The line frees about 90 px of the 192 px three departure rows took on the landscape walls (1.4 rows of 64), and every departure stops competing for room with the city.
3. **The time's short form.** Whole words (brief §5.3) applied to the time: below the design size (compact at zoom 0.8 gives a 144 px cell) "za 4 min" at the 44 px floor cannot fit. Decided per box and typography, never per minute, so the form does not flip. At compact 1366 x 768 the cell is 180 px and "za 10 min" 181 px before the `letter-spacing: -.01em` the titles already carry (about 177 after it): the accept scene records which form the box took.
4. **"Absent" headsign as an empty element.** The seam says `.k-dep-headsign` is absent with `data-headsign="0"`. The seam's reading ("no headsign on the wall", `data-headsign="0"`) holds.
5. **"The leading run"** becomes the first contiguous run: with the response policy's `railFirst` the trains stand before the departures (`nearby.ts:299`).

## Risks
- **The compact countdown at the edge** (0.5 item 3): 177 px of "za 10 min" in a 180 px cell. If the measured box takes the clock form, the compact wall shows blue clocks for its tracked trams. It is honest and one probe per box; the accept artefact says which form was taken, and the fix, if the owner wants countdowns there, is the cell gap (16 px is a §3 number the executor may change with a measured reason in the commit body).

- **A cell change must never recreate the li:** the paint removes departed cells before the keyed reconcile; a reorder of staying cells (a tram two minutes earlier climbing, `DISPLACE_MINUTES`) is a move of two records, as a row's is today.
- **The R0 merge in `dropCandidate`:** R0's value order may drop the reserved-line guard by rewriting the body; the "never returns the line" test fails on the integrated tree if it does (0.6).
- **The fitter's clone:** `measure.box(cell)` reads `scrollWidth` of a grid item; a browser that clips the nowrap time inside a `min-width: 0` cell without reporting it would hide an overflow. The accept scene's "no text of the line overflows its cell" reads the painted boxes, not the fitter's view.
