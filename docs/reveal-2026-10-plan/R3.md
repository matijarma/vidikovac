### 0.3 Decisions applied

- **D-A (brief D3).** The sources are the DHMZ radar composite, the biometeorological forecast, the heat and cold wave warnings, GPZ gas notices, and the school calendar beside R0's holidays. Nothing else is fetched.
- **D-B No new dependency.** `package.json` and `node_modules` hold no PNG library (`sharp` sits in `node_modules` only as a transitive dependency and runs only in Node, never in a Worker; no `pngjs`, no `upng-js`). The composite is an 8-bit RGB, non-interlaced PNG (probe P1), which a 150-line decoder over the platform's zlib streams reads. If the decoder fails on the real fixture for a reason the codec cannot cover in an hour (an interlaced or 16-bit image), the fallback is `upng-js` 2.1.0 as a runtime dependency: record that as a decision with the reason in the report.
- **D-C The fallback when decoding fails.** The module throws (the registry serves the last good copy as `stale` until `maxStale` 1800 s, then `down`); the route answers 503 with `cache-control: no-store`; no radar item exists while stale beyond its own `until` (10 min), so no row, no inset and no `rainNow` fact is said from an old image. An image whose size is not `calibration.imageSize`, or whose Last-Modified is more than 30 minutes before `ctx.now()`, is a failure of the same kind. Nothing anywhere says "no rain" from the radar: the absence of a radar fact is silence.
- **D-D Tier and timing.** `dhmz-radar` open, ttl 300, maxStale 1800; `dhmz-bio` open, 3600 / 86400; `dhmz-waves` open, 3600 / 86400; GPZ inside `prekidi` (session, 3600 / 172800, `degradeOnSources: false`, its own 6 s race). All three DHMZ modules have ttl ≥ 300, so the five-minute cron warms them (`WARM_MODULES`, `worker/feed/registry.ts:186`).
- **D-E The teaser.** The three DHMZ modules are tier `open`, so `/api/teaser` already carries them through `OPEN_MODULES` (`worker/routes/feed.ts:56`, `[...OPEN_MODULES, ...TEASER_MODULES]`). **They are not added to `TEASER_MODULES`**, which would list them twice (§0.5 C-4). The client's `KIOSK_TEASER_MODULES` (`app/src/kiosk/local.ts:331-332`) does gain all three (it is the list of modules a failed teaser leaves as down placeholders).
- **D-F The level words.** DHMZ publishes four levels of heat danger ("nema opasnosti, umjerena opasnost, velika opasnost i vrlo velika opasnost", `meteo.hr/prognoze.php?section=prognoze_specp&param=toplinskival_5&el=toplinski_val_opsirnije`, read 30 Sep 2026) and colours them green, yellow, orange and red (the archived page of 22 Jul 2019 shows Zagreb yellow on four days). The files carry letters: `G` (seen in the heat file), `W` (seen in the cold file, and rendered by DHMZ's own cold page as a grey "0"). The table is `W 0, G 0, Y 1, O 2, R 3`; `1.`, `2.` and `3. stupanj` are umjerena, velika and vrlo velika opasnost, and level 0 is never said. A letter outside the table drops that station's item and is counted in `coverage` (a change of DHMZ's format is then visible, and no level is guessed).
- **D-G Stale wave files.** Out of season DHMZ leaves the last file in place (the cold file of 30 Sep was created on 24 Feb 2026, the heat file on 13 Sep with an empty section). A file is read only when its first day (`<datatime>` DDMMHH, the year from `<creationtime>`) is today or yesterday in Zagreb; otherwise the module returns no item for it (a successful empty answer, not a failure).
- **D-H `isSchoolHoliday`.** From NN 58/2026 (read 30 Sep 2026 at `https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2026_06_58_724.html`): lessons 7 Sep to 23 Dec 2026 and 7 Jan to 15 Jun 2027; breaks 24 Dec 2026 to 5 Jan 2027, 22 to 26 Feb 2027, 25 Mar to 2 Apr 2027, summer from 16 Jun 2027. `isSchoolHoliday(day)` is true inside a break (every day of it), on 6 Jan 2027 (between the break and the first school day), on any `isPublicHoliday` day from 7 Sep 2026 to 15 Jun 2027, and from 16 Jun to 31 Aug 2027; false on every other day of that school year (weekends included: a weekend is not a holiday) and false outside 2026-09-07..2027-08-31, where the table has no decision (a comment says the next decision is added in June 2027). R3 wires no consumer: an audience model is not in this round (§0.4).
- **D-I Wind.** Withheld item 7 asked for wind from `dhmz-now`: the phone's Vrijeme already prints it (`app/src/layers/zrak-i-nebo.ts:63-86`, `facts()`, "Vjetar", with an arrow) and so does the paired weather hero. There is no wind string in the brief §7, so no wind family is added. Nothing to build.
- **D-J KGZ multi-day listings.** A listing whose `date_dog` is a range `DD.MM.YYYY. - DD.MM.YYYY.` that is not recurring and spans at most 31 days becomes an item with `precision: 'day'` (`at` the first day 00:00, `until` the day after the last, both Zagreb); a one-day listing without a readable time becomes a `precision: 'day'` item of that day. Recurring listings (a weekday word or "svak") and ranges longer than 31 days (library seasons such as "Pričaonica" 29.09.2026 to 29.06.2027) stay dropped and counted. The id keeps its form, `programi:kgz:<listing id>:<first day>`.
- **D-K The kids chip.** `data.kids` exists on `kultura-zg` items (`worker/feed/modules/kultura-zg.ts:132`); the chip filters to `kids === true`. It is shown only when at least one listed item carries `kids: true`.
- **D-L The radar inset size.** The composite is about 1 km a pixel (probe P1: 81.4 px per degree of longitude and 117.5 px per degree of latitude around Zagreb), so a 30 km square is 33 pixels. The image the route serves is the **120-pixel square around Zagreb** (about 115 km, native pixels), shown at 240 × 240 CSS px (`calc(240px * var(--k-zoom))`) with `image-rendering: pixelated`, the brief's size; `rainNear` is counted on the 30 km square inside it. A 33-pixel crop blown up eight times shows no geography.

## Risks

- **DHMZ changes the composite** (size, projection, colours): the size check and the calibration's `--check` catch the first two, and a changed scale makes rain pixels fail the rule (silence, never a false claim). The module then serves stale for 30 minutes and goes down; the inset and the fact disappear with it.
- **The rule misses light rain** at the edge of the scale (0,1 mm/h is `0044ff`, spread 255, so it counts); a threshold of 22 pixels in the square may call scattered drizzle "u blizini" or miss a single small cell. The executor tunes `share` against a rainy capture if one is had; the near square and the threshold are in the calibration file, so a change is one number.
- **The Worker's CPU.** One decode is about 1 MB of inflate and 300 rows of unfiltering; measure the test's decode time and, if a single decode exceeds 30 ms in Node, cut `rows` further (the module needs 244) and record it.
- **Two downloads of 380 KB every five minutes** (the cron's module fetch and the route's miss) when the isolates differ; `If-Modified-Since` reduces the second to a 304 in the same isolate. If DHMZ's server shows strain or answers 429, raise the radar ttl to 600 s and the route's `max-age` with it.
